7/*
8 * Design decision: why RelayGroup exists instead of EventGroup itself
9 * inheriting EventObserver.
10 *
11 * If every composite node were an observer, every group in the tree would
12 * carry the Observer machinery whether or not it ever takes part in a
13 * notification cascade, and it would be unclear which nodes are meant to
14 * be attached to a subject. Splitting the role into this subclass keeps
15 * plain EventGroup purely structural and makes the cascade topology
16 * explicit in the types: only RelayGroup instances are ever attached
17 * anywhere (e.g. to EventControl), and a plain EventGroup cannot be —
18 * it simply is not an EventObserver, so attach() won't accept it.
19 * Attaching one RelayGroup therefore covers its whole subtree with a
20 * single attach() call, while groups outside any cascade stay untouched.
21 */
